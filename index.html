ESP Multitool

Connected multi-tool firmware for the ESP32-D0WD with an SSD1306 OLED.

ESP32-D0WD SSD1306 OLED Wi-Fi · QR onboarding Web remote

Flash from your browser

Connect your ESP32-D0WD over USB and click below. Flashing uses Web Serial, so you need Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or another Chromium-based browser on desktop.

Your browser does not support Web Serial — try Chrome or Edge on desktop. Connecting to the device was not allowed.

If the device is not detected: hold BOOT, tap RST, release BOOT, then click Install.

Features

  • Wi-Fi onboarding by QR — the OLED shows a QR code; scan it with your phone to join the setup AP and enter your home Wi-Fi.
  • Web remote control — once online, a built-in web UI lets you control the device, upload files (IR code sets, images, config) and do OTA firmware updates.
  • IR — record and replay remotes, send stored IR codes.
  • Bluetooth LE — scan nearby BLE devices (name / MAC / RSSI).
  • NRF24 scanner — 2.4 GHz channel / spectrum monitor and passive sniffer (receive only).
  • Local control — three-button OLED menu (Up / Down / Select).

Receive / scan / own-device control only — no jamming, deauth or spam. For education and authorized testing; you are responsible for lawful use.

After flashing

  1. Press RST. The OLED shows Wi-Fi Setup with a QR code.
  2. Scan the QR with your phone to join the ESP-MT setup network, then enter your home Wi-Fi.
  3. The device reboots and shows a QR with its IP — scan it to open the web remote.
  4. From then on you can update over the air from the web UI (no cable needed).

First flash must be over USB. If flashing drops out, disconnect the NRF24 module and retry (it can cause power dips).
